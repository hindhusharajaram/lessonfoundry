# Market Sizing — India AI-in-Education

## TAM (Total Addressable Market)

India's AI-in-education market is valued at **$3.63 billion in 2025** and projected to reach **$33.31 billion by 2034**. This covers all AI-powered tools used across K-12, higher education, coaching, test prep, and corporate training — including adaptive learning platforms, content generation, intelligent tutoring, and administrative automation.

## SAM (Serviceable Available Market)

The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 mandates AI and computational thinking integration into the school curriculum starting from **Class 3 onward, with rollout beginning in the 2026-27 academic year**. Our serviceable market is the subset of Indian schools and coaching institutes that will actively adopt AI-powered content creation tools to meet this mandate — specifically those needing to produce curriculum-aligned, evidence-verified learning materials at scale. This includes CBSE, ICSE, and state board-affiliated schools, plus the organised coaching sector preparing students for board and competitive examinations. We do not have a reliable independent estimate for this segment's dollar value and will not fabricate one.

## SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

A realistic first-year target is **5–10 pilot school partnerships** or **one state education board collaboration** to deploy LessonFoundry for a specific subject and grade band (e.g., Class 6–8 Science under a single state board). The goal in year one is not revenue scale — it is generating auditable evidence that the trust pipeline reduces teacher preparation time and improves content accuracy, producing the case studies needed to expand to broader adoption.
