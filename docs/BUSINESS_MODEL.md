# Business Model

## Why B2B2C, Not Freemium

MagicSchool AI has already won the individual-teacher freemium race with 60+ tools and millions of sign-ups. Competing on that axis means fighting for the same single-teacher adoption funnel against a well-funded incumbent with a multi-year head start.

LessonFoundry's core value — an auditable trust pipeline with approval gating, version history, and Pack Health scoring — is not something an individual teacher shopping for a free quiz generator cares about. It is exactly what a **school principal, a coaching institute director, or a state education board** cares about when they need to guarantee that AI-generated content meets curriculum standards and can survive an external audit.

We sell to institutions, not individuals. Teachers are end users, not buyers.

## Revenue Model: Per-Institution Licensing

### Tier 1 — School License

- Unlimited teacher seats within a single school
- Full generation pipeline: source ingestion, blueprint planning, evidence-locked generation, guardrails, Pack Health scoring
- Student assessment portal with server-side grading
- Standard support

### Tier 2 — Audit & Compliance License

Everything in Tier 1, plus:

- **Exportable audit logs**: decision history, approval chains, version diffs, and claim-level provenance reports — formatted for submission to board inspectors or accreditation bodies
- **Admin dashboard**: institution-wide analytics on content quality scores, flagged items, teacher review rates, and student outcome trends
- **Multi-department deployment** with role-based access beyond teacher/student (HOD, principal, board coordinator)
- Priority support and onboarding

### Tier 3 — State Board / District Partnership

- Custom curriculum framework integration (CBSE, ICSE, state board syllabi)
- Bulk deployment across schools within a district or board
- API access for integration with existing state education portals or LMS platforms
- Dedicated implementation team

## Why This Works for Us

| Feature | Value to an Individual Teacher | Value to an Institution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Evidence-locked quotes | Nice to have | Audit-ready proof that content isn't hallucinated |
| Approval gating | Unnecessary overhead | Accountability chain from teacher to principal |
| Version history with diffs | Rarely used | Required for curriculum revision tracking |
| Pack Health Score (0–100) | Interesting metric | KPI for department-level content quality |
| Decision logs | Ignored | Compliance evidence for board inspections |
| Guardrail flags | Helpful warnings | Institutional risk management |

The pattern is clear: features that feel like friction to a solo teacher become **purchasing justifications** for an administrator responsible for quality assurance across dozens of teachers and thousands of students.

## Go-to-Market Sequence

1. **Pilot** (Year 1): 5–10 schools or one state board partnership, offered at reduced cost in exchange for case study rights and feedback access.
2. **Prove** (Year 1–2): Publish measurable outcomes — teacher hours saved, content accuracy rates, student performance data from pilot cohorts.
3. **Expand** (Year 2+): Use pilot evidence to sell Tier 2 and Tier 3 contracts to school chains and state boards through direct institutional sales, not bottoms-up teacher adoption.
